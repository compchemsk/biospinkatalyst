research

Computational chemistry in molecular biophysics

about

I like to apply computational methods in new ways to biophysics, chemistry and other computationally heavy fields.

My doctoral work at IIT Kharagpur, in the Quantum Simulation of Molecules Laboratory of Prof. Sabyashachi Mishra, combines classical molecular dynamics, enhanced sampling, Markov state models and quantum-chemical methods to explain how enzymes and catalysts work at the atomic level. Much of it centres on human iodotyrosine deiodinase, the flavoprotein in the thyroid gland that recycles iodide by reductive dehalogenation. Lately the work has turned to spin, asking where decoherence comes from in molecular qubits.

PhD thesis defence (tentative): 13 January 2027

Biomolecular simulation and QM/MM modelling of enzyme catalysis

How conformational motion, mutation and weak interactions shape catalysis in flavin-dependent and bacterial enzymes.

  • Flavin-mediated reductive deiodination in human iodotyrosine deiodinase (hIYD)
  • Active-site and distal mutations behind congenital hypothyroidism
  • Halogen-bonding-like contacts working alongside hydrogen bonds
  • Diaminopimelate epimerase dynamics and the L-lysine pathway as an antibiotic target

independent research · bioRxiv preprint · 2026

From Wandering Thoughts to Mental Stillness

A minimal computational statistical-mechanical model of driven relaxation

A new line of work that carries the tools of molecular simulation over to the mind. A latent tendency toward spontaneous thought moves on a tilted double-well landscape, shaped by a slowly relaxing record of attention. Simulations show that stronger focusing makes the quiet state more likely and shortens first-passage times, with a sharp change near a threshold, while noise acts non-monotonically. The model makes testable predictions and shows that entropy measures alone cannot identify thoughtlessness.

quantum information

From molecules to qubits

Using the tools of open quantum systems to understand how molecular spins can store quantum information, how they lose it, and how to prepare them in a known state.

  • Noise in molecular spin qubits. Atomistic simulations of a self-assembled monolayer of TEMPO radicals on gold, tracing where decoherence comes from at the microscopic level.
  • Qubit initialisation by controlled dissipation. Lindblad master-equation models of spin-½ and multi-level systems, simulated with QuTiP, showing how intersystem crossing, vibrational cooling and spin–phonon coupling drive population into a single ground-state sublevel.
  • Training and community. NSM Workshop on Quantum Computing (2026), Gordon Research Conference on Magnetic Resonance and Quantum Information Science (2026), and the Best Poster Award at Spins in Molecular Systems (2025).

Finds and characterises halogen bonds in proteins, from a single crystal structure to a full molecular dynamics trajectory.

Finds and characterises halogen bonds in proteins, from a single crystal structure to a full molecular dynamics trajectory.

  2. VerifyQuantum-chemical check through NBO second-order E(2) energies, from QM-cluster or QM/MM single points
  3. DecomposeALMO-EDA in Q-Chem separates polarisation from charge transfer
  4. PlotPublication-quality figures straight from the analysis

open code

Code and data on GitHub

Simulation inputs, analysis scripts and tools behind the papers.

Human_IYD_Dynamics

Dynamics of human IYD, a thyroid flavoprotein, using extra-point-charge force fields, molecular dynamics, conceptual DFT and QM-cluster calculations.

simulation inputs Human_IYD_Halogen_Bonding

Non-equilibrium simulations (steered MD, DTW, umbrella sampling) probing transient halogen bonding in hIYD.

Python Human_IYD_Congenital_Hypothyroidism

How subtle mutations in hIYD disrupt its dynamic network, changing structure, stability and substrate binding.

simulation inputs Metal_Catalyst_Design

DFT studies of reaction mechanisms and active sites, used to tune catalyst structures for polymerisation.

DFT Noise_In_Qubits

Atomistic modelling of a TEMPO spin-qubit monolayer on gold.

Python BondX-Studio

BondX Studio, an open-source toolkit for analysing halogen bonds in structures and trajectories.
